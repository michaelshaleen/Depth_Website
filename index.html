Meet Your Therapist

Hi, I'm Michael

I'm the founder of Depth Massage, with over 6 years of professional experience helping busy people reduce stress, melt away tension, and feel truly restored. After graduating from the Cortiva Institute of Massage Therapy in 2018, I dedicated my career to high-quality, personalized bodywork.

I created Depth Massage to give you convenient access to luxury-level therapeutic massage — either at your place or in my calm, private suite in St. Louis Park. Every session is customized to you: I listen carefully and blend techniques — from deep tissue to craniosacral therapy — to deliver noticeable results.
